# Chapter 1 - How We Got Here

**Source:** Chapter 1, PDF pages 18-26.

## Job of this chapter
Establish why the offer is the first commercial lever to examine before adding more marketing complexity.

## Working ideas
- A transaction begins with an offer: what the customer receives, what they give in exchange, and the terms around the exchange.
- Better acquisition does not rescue an unattractive or unprofitable offer for long.
- The author frames offer-making as a learnable skill improved through repeated attempts and feedback.
- A strong offer can materially change the economics of acquiring customers because it can improve response, conversion, and cash collected.

## Use this chapter when
- The user wants to fix acquisition but has not examined the actual offer.
- The user is adding channels, ads, or sales tactics to a weak package.
- The user needs to define exactly what is being exchanged.

## Decision rules
1. Write the offer in one sentence before discussing promotion.
2. Identify exactly what the buyer gets, pays, and commits to.
3. If the offer is vague, fix that before optimizing distribution.
4. Treat offer creation as iterative; a failed version is information for the next version.

## Avoid
- Treating the offer as a product list.
- Assuming more traffic automatically solves poor economics.
- Hiding weak value behind copywriting.
