# Glossary — $100M Offers

These are concise working definitions used by the skill.

## Offer
The goods/services, payment, and terms proposed in exchange for the customer's money.

## Grand Slam Offer
A highly differentiated offer designed to be difficult to compare directly, with strong perceived value, premium-pricing potential, risk reversal, and compelling terms.

## Commodity / Commoditized Offer
An offer buyers perceive as interchangeable with alternatives, pushing the decision toward price.

## Category of One
A position where the buyer cannot make an easy direct comparison and instead evaluates the offer on its specific value.

## Price-to-Value Discrepancy
The perceived gap between what the buyer gives up and what they receive.

## Starving Crowd
A market with strong existing demand for the desired solution.

## Massive Pain
A problem or unmet desire important enough to motivate action.

## Purchasing Power
The target market's ability to pay the price required for the business model to work.

## Easy to Target
The target audience can be identified and reached through clear channels, lists, groups, communities, locations, or media.

## Dream Outcome
The end state, experience, capability, relief, status, or result the buyer wants.

## Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
How strongly the buyer believes this offer will work for them.

## Time Delay
The time between starting and receiving meaningful value or the desired result.

## Effort & Sacrifice
The work, inconvenience, complexity, uncertainty, behavior change, time burden, or other sacrifices the buyer expects.

## Value Equation
The framework for increasing value by increasing Dream Outcome and Perceived Likelihood while decreasing Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice.

## Fast Win
An early milestone that gives the buyer evidence they are moving toward the larger result.

## Divergent Thinking
Generating many possible solutions before converging on the strongest option.

## Problems → Solutions
Mapping perceived obstacles on the customer journey and converting each obstacle into a solution or deliverable.

## Delivery Vehicle
The method used to deliver a solution: one-to-one, group, one-to-many, DIY, done-with-you, done-for-you, live, recorded, tool, support channel, and so on.

## 10x / 1/10th Test
Ask what you would deliver at ten times the price, then what high value could still be delivered at one-tenth the price. Use the contrast to generate delivery ideas.

## Sales-to-Fulfillment Continuum
The trade-off between making an offer easier to sell and making it easier to fulfill.

## Trim & Stack
Remove low-value items, especially those with high cost, then combine the remaining high-value solutions into the final offer.

## Scarcity
A real quantity constraint such as limited seats, slots, units, access, or bonuses.

## Urgency
A real time constraint such as a deadline, cohort start, scheduled price/bonus change, or time-decaying opportunity.

## Bonus
An additional high-perceived-value item used to solve an objection or widen the price-to-value gap.

## Guarantee
A risk-reversal mechanism specifying what happens if a defined result or condition is not met.

## Unconditional Guarantee
A guarantee with few or no buyer-performance conditions.

## Conditional Guarantee
A guarantee that applies when the buyer completes specified actions or requirements.

## Anti-Guarantee
A clearly stated all-sales-final position supported by a credible reason.

## Implied Guarantee
A performance-linked commercial structure where payment itself depends on results.

## M-A-G-I-C Naming
A naming framework using some combination of Magnetic Reason Why, Avatar, Goal, Interval, and Container.
